Frequently asked questions

Shelob Web monitors SEO health, Core Web Vitals performance, accessibility compliance, AI readiness, and broken links across all your websites. It integrates with Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console, and Cloudflare for comprehensive insights.
After you add a website, we run an instant audit of your homepage. Then a full-site crawl runs automatically every night, checking every page in your sitemap for SEO issues, accessibility problems, broken links, and AI readiness signals. Your dashboard updates each morning with the latest results.
AI readiness scoring measures how well your website works with AI search engines and chatbots like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Claude. It checks for llms.txt files, AI bot access in robots.txt, structured data depth, content clarity, and client-side rendering issues that can hide your content from AI crawlers.
Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of structuring your website so AI answer engines — like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Claude — can find, understand, and cite your content. Where traditional SEO optimizes for ranked links, AEO optimizes for being quoted directly in AI-generated answers. Shelob Web measures your AEO signals: FAQ and structured data markup, llms.txt presence, AI crawler access, concise answer formatting, author and date signals, and server-side rendered content.
SEO (Search Engine Optimization) aims to rank your pages in traditional search results so users click through to your site. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) aims to get your content extracted and cited inside AI-generated answers, where there may be no click at all. They overlap — clean structured data and fast, crawlable pages help both — but AEO adds requirements like llms.txt files, explicit AI bot permissions, FAQ schema, and content written in clear, self-contained answers. Shelob Web scores both.
